Privacy statement

What we collect

Only the answers you give in the questionnaire: a lecture selection, six ratings per lecture, and any free-text comments you choose to write. Alongside them we store the date of submission — not the time.

What we do not collect

No name, no e-mail address, no matriculation number, no IP address, no browser fingerprint, and no cookie that would let us recognise you. Responses are stored with a random identifier that carries no order information, so submissions cannot be linked to each other or to a person.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is used to evaluate the lecture series and to plan future programmes. Participation is voluntary; the legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by starting the questionnaire.

Where the data lives

On infrastructure operated for the graduate school. Nothing is passed to third-party processors, and the questionnaire loads no external resources — no fonts, scripts or analytics from other servers.

Retention and deletion

Responses are kept until the evaluation of the respective run is complete, then deleted. Because your response holds no identifier, an individual submission cannot be located and withdrawn after the fact; the organisers can, however, delete the entire data set.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since the data is anonymous, we may be unable to act on a request that concerns a single response.

Contact

Please contact the organisers of the Graduate Days. Replace this paragraph with the responsible person, postal address and the data protection officer of the institution before going live.
